# Findings

1. **Autumn festival months (Navratri-Dussehra and Diwali).** Festival months run only a few percent above trend. Main window (Apr 2021+), STL on log series. Total trend-adjusted festival lift (total = seasonal + remainder; remainder = the part left after the usual seasonal pattern): volume +2.6%, value +2.9%, ticket size +0.15%. Autumn-season remainder by year: volume -1.9% to +3.4%, value -2.0% to +3.8% (5 seasons). Sensitivity window (Jan 2019+): pooled autumn remainder volume +0.8%, value +2.9%, ticket size +1.7%; value ranges from -3.0% to +13.6% by year (2020 is the high). Limitation: 5-7 seasons; sign and size vary by year.

2. **Value responds to Diwali timing more than volume (suggestive, not established).** Oct-vs-Nov interaction: value +3.6% (main) / +3.4% (sensitivity); ticket size +2.4% / +2.0%; volume +0.6% / -0.9%. Limitation: 2-3 years per group; the group averages are driven by single years (value +4.6% in Oct 2024, +3.8% in Nov 2023); STL's learned seasonal partly builds this in.

3. **Simple models forecast monthly UPI well.** Rolling-origin backtest, 1-3 months ahead, MAPE on volume: ETS damped 1.1 / 1.2 / 1.4%; naive+growth 1.9 / 2.4 / 3.1%; no-growth floor about 25%. Oct/Nov months are harder (ETS volume 2.3% vs 0.9% elsewhere). Limitation: the same backtest selects the model, so errors are somewhat optimistic. In the 2019-2025 Sep-origin festival backtest used for the forecast ranges, ETS damped volume error averages about 3.5% (Oct 3.2%, Nov 3.8%), larger than in the main window because early years are less stable. Source: festival backtest in src/make_forecast.py (n=7 per month; pooled 3.52% is also in forecasts/README.md).

4. **Published forecast (before NPCI data), trained through Sep 2026.** Volume (ETS damped): Oct 25,164 mn (range 24,949-27,644), Nov 24,582 mn (23,953-28,283). Value (naive+growth): Oct Rs 32.47 lakh crore (29.70-33.69), Nov Rs 31.32 lakh crore (25.84-32.62). Ranges are empirical backtest error ranges (n=7), not 95% intervals. Limitation: unscored until NPCI publishes October and November data.

5. **One strong anomaly.** Oct 2024 volume +5.5% above the one-step forecast (z about 4) and value +8.9%; Diwali fell on 31 Oct 2024. Other flagged months are borderline (z 2.0-2.6); about 5% false flags are expected by chance. Limitation: With 30 months tested per series, one or two flags above |z|=2 are expected by chance.
